## 4. Assembly guide (CapCut / Canva / Premiere)

1. Timeline 1920x1080, 30 fps. Put clips and screens per the table above.
2. Screens: add 8-10% slow zoom (Ken Burns), 0.3s fade or push transitions. Add a soft shadow and rounded corners on a white or light gray background so they look like a product shot.
3. Text: Poppins SemiBold, ink #1C1D1F with amber #FDAE04 highlight words. Max 6 words per card. Keep text on screen at least 1.5s.
4. Music: upbeat light corporate (no vocals), around 100-110 BPM, duck under the VO. Add a soft whoosh on the logo reveal.
5. VO: record in Taglish, calm and friendly. About 10 seconds of VO per 20-25 words.
6. Export MP4 H.264, 1080p. Keep a version without VO for live presenting.
